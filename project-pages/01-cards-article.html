Markera

Scoring a precision-shooting series from a single phone photo of the target — every step on the device.

Kotlin MultiplatformAndroid · iOSActive2026

Markera turns a phone photo of a paper target into a scored series. A YOLOv8 model finds the bullet holes, and that part worked early and never really stopped working. The hard problem was geometry: a hole 40 mm from the centre is a 9 or an 8 depending on where the centre actually is.

Finding the centre

Paper targets have their ring values printed in a horizontal and a vertical row that cross at the centre. On-device OCR reads them, a line is fitted through each row, and the two lines are intersected.

Four probes walk to the rim

Instead of fitting the whole ring boundary, four probe disks start where the digits predict the 6/7 ring and settle onto the black-to-white rim. An ellipse through them undoes the perspective.

The full story of the seven approaches is in the detection write-up.

The problem

Which ring is it, really?

Holes are easy to find. Whether a hole is a 9 or an 8 depends on exactly where the centre is and how much the camera tilted — and a paper target is covered in look-alike circles.

The approach

Let the printed digits decide

OCR reads the ring numbers, their rows cross at the centre, and four probe disks walk from the predicted 6/7 radius to the actual rim. Seven approaches were tried; three pieces survived.

The result

40 of 42 scans on the rim

First run on a real phone: every probe landed on the rim in 40 of 42 scans. Series sync, get tagged by caliber and feed a statistics screen.

What it does

Point the phone at a paper target, take one photo, and the series is scored: holes found, rings measured, perspective undone. Everything runs on the device.

How scoring works

A YOLOv8 model boxes the holes. OCR reads the printed ring digits; their two rows cross at the centre. Four probe disks settle on the 6/7 rim and an ellipse through them gives scale and tilt.

The app

Series are tagged by caliber (24 of them, which also scale the hit overlay), synced to a backend, grouped by day, and plotted on a zoomable drawn target with mean and median impact markers.

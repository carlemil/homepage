README

Markera

Score precision-shooting series from a single photo of the target. Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose for Android and iOS.

How it works

  • Holes — on-device YOLOv8 ONNX model.
  • Centre — OCR of the printed ring digits (ML Kit / Apple Vision), two fitted rows intersected.
  • Scale — four probe disks settle on the 6/7 rim; an ellipse through them undoes perspective.

Features

  • 24 calibers, free-text tags
  • Synced history grouped by day, zip export
  • Statistics: zoomable target, mean/median impact, trends ±1 SD
  • Light/dark theme, language switch

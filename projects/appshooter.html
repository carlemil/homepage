The app

Competitions in your pocket

Lets shooting-club members browse competitions, sign up, and follow results, patrols and score trends — one shared Compose UI across Android and iOS, with offline caching.

Club statistics and charts of series points sit beside the competitions. The app ships with a pre-filled database, so the first screen has content straight away, and it paces its requests to stay within webshooter.se's rate limit.

The testing

Over 700 tests across both platforms

About 620 JVM tests run on the Android side, many of them guards on the app's structure, such as stable list keys, a preview for every screen and the sign-in secret kept out of tracked files. 96 shared tests cover the mappers, the repository sync and the rate limiter, and both platforms run them.

27 Compose UI tests drive login, settings, the drawer, competition details and the mock mode on Android, and 37 XCTest cases do the same on iOS, among them the mock login used for store review.

A booking app for football training sessions, on the web, Android and iOS.

PlatformsWeb · Android · iOS
Started2026
StatusActive2026-10-26
The app

Register, pre-book, pay

Parents sign in with Google or Apple, register their players and pre-book sessions. Each pre-booking is held for 60 minutes, and a card payment through Stripe confirms it. Admins manage the schedule, prices, users, players and bookings, and coaches see their sessions and player rosters. The apps speak Swedish, English and Turkish.

The stack

One API, three clients

The backend is a Rust API built on axum and sqlx over PostgreSQL, with row-locked capacity so a race for the last places ends with exactly the right number of bookings. The web, Android and iOS clients share one Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform codebase, checked against golden responses from the API contract.

The deployment

Docker Compose and Caddy

The stack runs in Docker Compose behind Caddy, with separate test and production sites. Continuous WAL archiving ships encrypted backups offsite, and a restore drill checks them end to end.

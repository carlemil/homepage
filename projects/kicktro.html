The effects

55 of them, four layers deep

Fractal fire, plasma, copper and Kefrens bars, raymarched Mandelbulbs and Menger sponges — stackable four layers deep with per-layer palettes and filters, and beat-reactive to whatever music you're playing.

The gallery

Publish a scene

Scenes can be published to a public gallery anyone can browse, and a built-in AI-generated collection ships with the app.

The river

Ten stages to the sea

The river drives; you steer and work the throttle. The run passes through ten stages, from Bokeo in Laos through Luang Prabang, Vientiane, the braided channels of Si Phan Don, Phnom Penh and Cần Thơ, out to the South China Sea. Each stage has its own banks, towns, boats and hazards: fishing nets, snags, barges, sandbars and eddies. The speed climbs from 52 to 160 km/h, and the passengers you carry weigh the boat down.

The highscores

One river a week

Everyone sails the same river each week, and the world boards rank the best attempts, the finished runs and the daily champions. The game is deterministic, so the server replays each run from its seed and inputs and ranks only the score it computed itself.

The build

A pure simulation under three.js

The river is a channel graph grown from a grammar of reaches: bends, hairpins, open water and splits round islands that always rejoin. A fixed 120 Hz simulation holds the game rules apart from the three.js renderer, and the engine sound and effects are synthesised in the browser.

The river

Eleven stages to the sea

The river drives; you steer and work the throttle. The run passes through eleven stages, from Bokeo in Laos through Luang Prabang, Vientiane, the braided channels of Si Phan Don, Phnom Penh and Cần Thơ, out to the South China Sea. Each stage has its own banks, towns, boats and hazards: fishing nets, snags, barges, sandbars and eddies. The river carries the boat at 52 km/h the whole way down; wide open, the engine climbs slowly towards 470 km/h, and the passengers you carry weigh the boat down, so a full boat of six tops out at 400.

The highscores

One river a week

Everyone sails the same river each week, and the world boards rank the best attempts, the finished runs and the daily champions. The game is deterministic, so the server replays each run from its seed and inputs and ranks only the score it computed itself. Any ranked run can be raced as a ghost, replayed by the build of the game that sailed it.

The pickups

Boosters and eleven pickups on the water

Boosters float on the river, one for top speed and one for acceleration, and stack. Among them float eleven pickups, each with its own meter: a lifebuoy that reels a lost passenger back aboard, a ram prow that smashes small boats and cuts through nets, a sticky deck, a slipstream ribbon along the clear path, a monk's blessing that forgives the next hit, a fog lamp, a spare engine, a hydrofoil that steers in the air, a magnet for pickups and waiting passengers, a ghost hull that passes through everything for five seconds, and a rare hourglass that takes two seconds off the ranked time. The Guide shows each one with a picture and a line, in all four of the game's languages: English, Lao, Khmer and Thai.

The build

A pure simulation under three.js

The river is a channel graph grown from a grammar of reaches: bends, hairpins, open water and splits round islands that always rejoin. A fixed 120 Hz simulation holds the game rules apart from the three.js renderer, and the engine sound and effects are synthesised in the browser.

The testing

Bit-exact replays, checked every push

63 Vitest files test the simulation, the river, the spawner, the boat, the pickups, the weekly boards and the server. The central one pins determinism: a run's seed and its steer and throttle per tick must replay to exactly the same result, since the server ranks only what it replays itself. Scripted captains sail the tests, one an autopilot, one that rams hazards on purpose. Every push to main runs the tests and the build before the game is published to GitHub Pages.

The look is checked in the real game: 35 headless check scripts drive it in a browser and save screenshots of birds, dams, pickups, ghosts, the languages and more, and a frame profiler measures what each frame costs on the real GPU at set spots along the river, logged pass by pass.

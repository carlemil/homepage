The idea

One line, thick where it's dark

A camera app that redraws the live viewfinder as a single space-filling L-system curve, varying the line's thickness with image brightness.

The rebuild

19 curves, two platforms

Rebuilt in 2026 on Compose Multiplatform (a shared Kotlin module, CameraX on Android, AVFoundation on iOS), it ships 19 curves — Hilbert, Moore, Gosper, Dragon, Terdragon, FASS and more — with complexity, contrast and brightness controls, camera switching, and sharing.

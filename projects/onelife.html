The game

Talk your way through

A browser-played text adventure where you talk your way past AI-driven characters in free text — one LLM speaks in character while a separate referee judges whether you earned the gate.

The rule

One life

Your log is your progress: death is real, and rolling it back costs you on the leaderboard.

The world

Three games, one lobby

Three games run side by side behind a lobby, each with its own save. Every location gets a generated image and a soundtrack the model picks from Spotify, the world and its cities have maps to travel by, and the characters remember what players told them. Players sign in with email, password and a TOTP code. The characters run on Claude, or on a small model in the player's own browser through WebLLM.

The testing

Every story proved finishable

Each game's YAML passes a validating seeder before it loads: it checks every reference and proves that every place the story can reach still leads to an ending. A playtest script then walks every reachable state the way the engine offers choices, looking for soft-locks, dead content and broken references, and a second one plays the game live through the API to catch characters asking for things the story cannot follow.

The game

Talk your way through

A browser-played text adventure where you talk your way past AI-driven characters in free text — one LLM speaks in character while a separate referee judges whether you earned the gate.

The rule

One life

Your log is your progress: death is real, and rolling it back costs you on the leaderboard.

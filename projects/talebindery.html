The photos

Your photos, Claude's words

Every page shows one of your own photos, cropped and colour-corrected. Claude checks each upload, suggests crops, picks and orders up to 60 photos from as many as 200, and writes each page's words around them.

The book

Edit until it reads right, then print

Revise by instruction ("make the ending quieter"), rewrite or swap a single page, and reorder freely; every change is a new storyboard version, so the history is the undo. Buying the book unlocks the PDF, and Lulu prints and ships it as a hardcover, paperback or booklet. Next.js web, a Fastify API and BullMQ workers over Postgres and S3, with Claude writing the story and Stripe billing.

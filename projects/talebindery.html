TaleBindery

A roll of your own photos turned into a picture book, with the story written around them, one photo per page.

StackTypeScript, Next.js, Fastify, BullMQ, Postgres, S3
ModelsClaude, text only
BillingStripe, printing by Lulu
StatusActive
Linkstalebindery.com
The photos

Only the words are generated

No image is ever generated. Every page shows one of your own photos, and the only things done to it are cropping and colour correction. Claude reads the photos and writes: it checks each upload, suggests crops, picks and orders up to 60 photos from as many as 200, and writes each page's words around them.

The book

Edit until it reads right, then print

Revise by instruction ("make the ending quieter"), rewrite or swap a single page, and reorder freely; every change is a new storyboard version, so the history is the undo. Buying the book unlocks the PDF, and Lulu prints and ships it as a hardcover, paperback or booklet. Next.js web, a Fastify API and BullMQ workers over Postgres and S3, with Claude writing the text and Stripe billing.

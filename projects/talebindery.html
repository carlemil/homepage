The trade-off

Cheap text, deliberate renders

Storyboard text is cheap to revise and the image run is not, so the whole stack — Next.js web, Fastify API, BullMQ workers over Postgres and S3 — is built to keep iteration free and make the paid render step deliberate and pinned.

The characters

Recognizable on every page

Character sheets rendered from the photos keep each person recognizable page to page, and every picture a page was ever drawn stays available to choose from. Claude writes the story, Gemini draws it, Stripe bills it.
